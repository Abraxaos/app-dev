**Fight Club**
![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a779528a-00e5-4011-a8e9-721080b14f1b)
Fight Club is a 1996 novel by Chuck Palahniuk that was adapted into a movie. The story follows the unnamed narrator, who is discontented with his white-collar job, and forms a "fight club" with soap salesman Tyler Durden. The narrator becomes embroiled in a relationship with a destitute woman, Marla Singer. The story explores and exposes the dark heart of modern society, and contends with masculinity, materialism, consumer culture, and modern disillusionment.
